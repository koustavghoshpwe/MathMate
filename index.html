Privacy and cost

No paid API key, account, or subscription is used. The symbolic engine runs in your browser using Pyodide and SymPy. Initial loading downloads open-source libraries from public CDNs; internet is required for first setup and photo OCR. The app shell can be installed on Android as a PWA. Do not treat results as a substitute for checking important academic work.
